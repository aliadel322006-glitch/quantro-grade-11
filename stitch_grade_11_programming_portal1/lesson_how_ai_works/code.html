Worked Example

Let's see a simple example of how an AI system learns from data.

Example: Email Spam Detection

An AI model is trained using many examples of emails labeled as "Spam" or "Not Spam".

The model learns patterns such as:

  • Certain words (e.g., "Free", "Winner")
  • Suspicious links
  • Sender behavior

Then it can predict whether a new email is spam or not.

Step-by-Step

1

Collect data

Gather many emails and label them.

2

Train the model

The AI finds patterns in the labeled data.

3

Make a prediction

The model predicts if a new email is spam.
